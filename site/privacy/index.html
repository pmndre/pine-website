Privacy: the Pine browser extension

The Pine extension lets the agents of pine, a program you run on your own computer, work in your browser: in a tab group of their own, in the tabs they open there and the tabs you drag into it.

What it sends, and where

Nothing to us. The extension has no server. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no remote code. We receive nothing from it.

To pine on your own computer, and nowhere else. The extension talks to one program: pine's host, which your browser starts on your computer (native messaging). Nothing leaves your computer through the extension.

What it handles

Only the tabs in pine's tab groups. For those tabs, the extension passes to pine what an agent asks for (the page's content, pictures of the page, what the page logs) and carries out what the agent does there (opening an address, clicks, typing). A tab outside pine's groups is never read or touched. Dragging a tab out of a group, or pressing Cancel on the browser's "started debugging this browser" bar, takes it back at once.

What pine does with a page an agent read is pine's own: it gives it to the AI model you set up in pine. If that is a model of a provider on the internet, the provider receives what the agent read, under that provider's terms. With a model on your own computer, it stays there.

In those tabs an agent is signed in wherever you are, because it is your browser. The extension does not read, copy or store your passwords or cookies.

What it keeps

In the browser's own extension storage, on your computer: which tab group belongs to which conversation, which tabs an agent opened itself, and one version number. No page content and nothing about you. It is gone when you remove the extension.

Permissions

debugger
To read and operate the pages in pine's tab groups, as an agent's browser does, and to draw the agent's pointer there.
tabGroups
To make a tab group for a conversation and know which tabs are in it.
nativeMessaging
To talk to pine on your computer.
storage
To remember which group is whose while the browser runs.
alarms
To look for pine again when it was not running.

The extension asks for no access to sites and is not told the addresses of your tabs. What it does in a page goes through the debugger, which it attaches only to the tabs in pine's tab groups.

Changes and contact

This text is kept with the extension's source, as extension/PRIVACY.md in pine's repository, where its history shows every change. Questions and reports: github.com/pmndre/pine/issues.
